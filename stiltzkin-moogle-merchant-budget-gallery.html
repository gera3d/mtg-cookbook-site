How to run the shop

Play Stiltzkin on turn one. Once he is untapped, make the opponent who is least able to absorb the permanent take it: Steel Golem closes off creature spells, the Masticores demand cards, Torture Chamber and Voodoo Doll turn time into pain, and the Jinxed objects demand an answer. Stiltzkin draws you a card only when the hand-off actually succeeds, so do not lead with a harmless gift when you have a real cursed item.

  • Pressure: use the wide token package—Akroan Horse, Martial Coup, White Sun’s Zenith, Decree of Justice, Captain of the Watch, and Myr Battlesphere—then amplify it with Intangible Virtue, Glorious Anthem, or Goldnight Commander.
  • Stay alive: hold the real removal for the permanent that breaks the game, not every creature. The deck has enough awkward gifts to redirect attacks and buy turns.
  • Recover: Late to Dinner and Sanctum Gargoyle get back an actual workhorse; Blacksmith’s Skill and Emerge Unscathed protect Stiltzkin or a finishing board.
