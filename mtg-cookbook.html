The skill can price. You decide when to buy.

Building a decklist and quoting it live against CardTrader Zero is safe research — the skill does that on its own. Touching a live cart, accepting a substitution, or paying are separate, explicit actions. It states the exact total and waits for a fresh confirmation immediately before checkout, every single time. No cart, wishlist, or order gets created on an un-purchased batch.
