Why this is a different deck

Omens are cheap copy targets. Copy Omen of the Sea for cards, Omen of the Sun for bodies, Omen of the Hunt for mana, or Omen of the Forge for immediate damage.
Sagas are the burst mode. Terra can fast-forward an Eldest Reborn, Cruelty of Gix, Cerberus, or Fenrir token when the table gives her time to transform.
Constellation closes games. Archon, Ajani’s Chosen, Doomwake Giant, and Grim Guardian make even the low-cost enchantments matter after the first cast.

Color and legality

Five-color permission, green-red start

The deck opens mostly on Forests and Mountains, then uses the remaining colors for Sagas and interaction. Terra’s back face makes her Commander color identity WUBRG because its fourth chapter produces each color of mana.

What stays cheap on purpose

The top of the curve is composed of low-cost Sagas, not premium staples. The deck accepts a lower ceiling to preserve a coherent game.
