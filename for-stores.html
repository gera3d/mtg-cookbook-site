the sequencing that matters

Own your inventory record first. Then use Zero for reach.

The skill behind this whole site runs on one hard rule: card-rules truth and marketplace pricing are two different sources, and they never get mashed together. The same discipline is worth applying to a store's own stock.

  • Keep your own record of SKU, condition, and quantity — something you wouldn't lose access to if a platform relationship ever changed
  • Compute pricing from a rule (cost + margin, or live comp data), not hand-typed per listing
  • Zero then becomes an additive reach channel for the bottom of that inventory — the $0.10–$0.50 stock nobody wants to list one card at a time — not the only copy of your data

Zero mechanics — consolidated weekly shipment to one hub, free to start, 3,000+ active sellers — are from CardTrader's own seller page: cardtrader.com/en/pages/zero_sellers.
