Ryu, World Warrior is the clearly matching Magic card: a Commander-legal red-white legendary creature. Exact Ryu had no live English CardTrader Zero listing in this snapshot. The $16.70 build therefore uses Vikya, Scorching Stalwart, the mechanically identical Commander-legal Universes Within printing, at $0.29. That is a visible purchase choice, not a silent substitution. Verify the Ryu/Vikya card identity and legality.

How it plays: deploy cheap mana and a bigger attacker, cast Ryu/Vikya, and attack with the larger body to train him. Equipment makes both the training line and combat meaningful. After combat, Hadoken turns Ryu’s power into targeted damage; excess damage draws a card. The support cards either make bodies, reduce/ease Equipment, protect Ryu, or keep cards and mana flowing. This is combat Commander, not cEDH—but every card pulls toward the same plan.
