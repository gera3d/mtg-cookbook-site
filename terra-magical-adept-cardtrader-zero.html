What Terra actually rewards

Start green-red. Fourteen ramp pieces, nine Forests, and five Mountains make Terra and her Trance activation the early mana job. The other colors arrive later.
Burst a real Saga. On Esper Terra’s first three chapters, copy a nonlegendary enchantment. If it is a Saga, it gets up to three more lore counters right away—so the right target pays off now.
Turn copies into a win. Weaver of Harmony multiplies chapter triggers; Eidolon, Archon, Doomwake, and Grim Guardian turn the copied enchantments into cards, bodies, removal, and damage.

Legal commander check

WUBRG color identity

Terra starts red-green, but Esper Terra’s chapter IV creates white, blue, black, red, and green mana. The color identity is therefore five-color; the deck is allowed to use every listed card even though its early turns favor green and red.

Oracle source: the official FINAL FANTASY release notes confirm the copy-and-lore-counter interaction. The current Commander banned list is the official Wizards list.
